MOTTO Automotive

Designed for the moment the customer receives the keys

MOTTO develops custom keychains for automotive dealer handovers, fleet delivery, campaigns and premium gifting in the UAE. Programmes can include custom leather keychains, metal formats or mixed-material designs, with approved artwork, comfortable hardware, edge finishing, presentation packaging and branch allocation. The production sample should be checked for logo execution, ring movement, handling comfort and appearance after normal use.

What to send us

  • Dealer or vehicle brand artwork

05 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can keychains be supplied in presentation boxes?

Yes. Box, insert, sleeve and branch labelling requirements can be developed as part of the programme.

Can one design support several vehicle brands?

A common construction can be adapted, but each artwork, colour and pack configuration should have its own approved master.

What should be checked on the sample?

Check logo execution, edge finish, ring and joint movement, handling comfort, pack fit and the appearance after normal handling.
