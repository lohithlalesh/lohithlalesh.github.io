Buyer answer

What car accessories does MOTTO manufacture in the UAE?

MOTTO Automotive is a B2B car accessories manufacturer serving dealer groups, fleets and mobility brands across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and the wider UAE. Its custom automotive accessories include number plate holders, 3D car badges and emblems, metal and leather keychains, windscreen sun shades, non-registration showroom plates and coordinated white-label programmes. Each project begins with the real vehicle application, approved brand artwork, quantity and delivery plan. MOTTO then controls the production-intent sample, material and finish specification, manufacturing, inspection, packaging and branch allocation. This process is designed for commercial buyers who need repeatable quality and reliable replenishment, not one-off retail items. Official registration plates remain the responsibility of the competent UAE licensing authority. MOTTO manufactures the accessory holder or display product around the approved application. Buyers can start with one product category or combine several items into a dealer handover, fleet or vehicle-launch programme.
