MOTTO Automotive

Dimensional branding developed as a complete system

MOTTO develops custom car badges and 3D automotive emblems from approved vector artwork through design review, forming or tooling, decorative finishing, attachment selection, sample approval, inspection and protective packing. The car emblem badge, adhesive and installation surface must be considered together, especially for vehicles exposed to Gulf heat, ultraviolet light, dust, washing and humidity.

01 / Specification

Specify the programme

01

Geometry and relief

Define outer shape, thickness, edge profile, raised details and the permitted installation envelope.

02

Surface finish

Approve chrome-style, metallic, coloured or mixed finishes against a physical master sample.

03

Attachment system

Match adhesive or mechanical attachment to the substrate, surface preparation and service environment.

04

Placement control

Use an approved location template and clear installer instructions to protect alignment and repeatability.

03 / Project brief

What to send us

  • Vector logo and brand guidelines
  • Vehicle panel material and installation position
  • Target dimensions, relief and finish
  • Environmental and cleaning expectations
  • Quantity, packaging and placement-template needs

Final adhesive and finish validation should use the intended vehicle surface, preparation method and service conditions. A sample approved only by appearance does not confirm installation performance.

05 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can MOTTO reproduce any logo as a badge?

Production begins only from artwork the customer is authorised to use and after the geometry and finish are technically reviewed.

How is badge placement kept consistent?

A location template, reference dimensions and installer instructions can be included so branches use the same approved position.

Should a badge sample be tested on the real panel?

Yes. The intended surface, cleaning method, adhesive and service conditions should be represented during validation.
