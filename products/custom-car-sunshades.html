Practical shade with a visible dealer message

MOTTO develops custom car sun shades for dealer handovers, fleet operations, seasonal campaigns and promotional programmes in the UAE. Each car window sun shade is specified around windscreen coverage, mirror and sensor clearance, reflective material behaviour, print legibility, folding, storage and pack-out. A production-intent car windshield sun shade should be tested inside representative vehicles before a mixed-model bulk order is released.

05 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can one size cover an entire fleet?

A size family may cover several vehicles, but representative fit trials are recommended before a mixed-fleet order.

Can the full sunshade be printed?

Print coverage depends on material, fold lines and the approved process. Safe zones should be confirmed on the production sample.

Should the sunshade be tested inside a real vehicle?

Yes. Check coverage, mirror and sensor clearance, dashboard contact, folding and storage in representative vehicles.
