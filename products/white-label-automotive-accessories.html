Your automotive accessory programme, engineered at source

MOTTO works with automotive organisations that need a coordinated accessory programme rather than a generic catalogue item. The project can cover product development, branding, production, inspection, packaging, branch labelling and recurring supply, with clear responsibility for approvals and revisions.

05 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What products can be included in a white-label programme?

Programmes can include plate holders, badges, keychains, sunshades, showroom products and other technically reviewed automotive accessories.

Who owns the approved specification?

Ownership and usage rights for artwork, tooling and technical files should be agreed in the project terms before production.

Can supply be divided between branches?

Yes. Branch allocation, call-off quantities, carton labels and replenishment rules can be built into the approved pack-out plan.
