MOTTO Automotive

Built around fit, visibility and dealer identity

MOTTO Automotive manufactures custom number plate holders in Dubai for luxury car brands, dealer delivery vehicles, fleet programmes and automotive campaigns across the UAE. Each car number plate holder programme starts with the official plate format and vehicle mounting area, then moves through artwork, polymer selection, branded finish, sample approval, production inspection and packaging. Buyers searching for custom number plates for luxury brands usually need a dealer-branded accessory holder or a non-registration showroom plate, not a replacement for the official plate. MOTTO manufactures the accessory holder only. Official UAE registration plates are issued by the competent licensing authority.

05 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does MOTTO manufacture official UAE registration plates?

No. MOTTO manufactures accessory plate holders and display products. Official registration plates are issued by the competent UAE licensing authority.

Can one holder fit every UAE vehicle?

A universal assumption is risky. Plate format, bumper recess, mounting points and front or rear position should be confirmed for the intended applications.

Can each dealer branch receive different packaging?

Yes, branch labels, carton quantities and rollout allocations can be included in the approved project specification.
