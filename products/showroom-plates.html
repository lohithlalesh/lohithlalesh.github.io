A consistent brand surface for every display vehicle

MOTTO manufactures custom showroom display plates for luxury automotive brands, UAE dealer floors, launch events, vehicle photography and promotional environments. These non-registration products can carry model names, campaign messages or dealer identity, with a controlled specification covering dimensions, substrate, print, finish, mounting and protective packaging. They do not replace official UAE registration plates.

05 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are showroom plates official registration plates?

No. They are display products for controlled showroom, event, photography or promotional use.

Can each vehicle model have a different face?

Yes. A shared construction can support multiple approved artworks, with each design controlled as a separate version.

Can plates be packed for repeated events?

Yes. Protective sleeves, separators, labelled sets and event-specific cartons can be included in the specification.
