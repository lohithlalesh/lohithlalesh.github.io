How to Order Custom Dealer-Branded Number Plate Holders

Learn how UAE dealers order custom number plate holders, from official plate and vehicle fit through artwork, samples, packaging and branch delivery.

By MOTTO AutomotiveAugust 16, 20264 min readReviewed August 21, 2026
Home/Insights/Dealer and B2B Use Cases/How to Order Custom Dealer-Branded Number Plate Holders
Quick answer

To order custom dealer-branded number plate holders, confirm the official plate format, vehicle list, mounting points, authorised artwork, branding process, order quantity and branch allocation. Approve a production-intent car number plate holder on representative vehicles before volume production, then lock the sample, packaging and reorder specification.

Custom number plate holders carry a dealership name on every delivered vehicle, so the order needs more control than a generic promotional item. The brief must connect brand standards with physical fit, manufacturing method, inspection and showroom handling. This step-by-step guide is designed for dealer marketing, procurement, aftersales and fleet teams in the UAE.

1. Define where and how the holders will be used

Start with the programme, not the decoration. Record whether the holders are for new-car handovers, used vehicles, fleet replacements, an event, a particular marque or a group-wide rollout. Confirm the issuing emirates, expected front and rear plate formats, vehicle models and annual volume.

This information tells the manufacturer whether one established holder will work or whether more than one fit variant is needed.

2. Build the fit specification

A purchasing description such as "standard UAE plate frame" is too vague for a controlled order. Include the actual official plate format, front or rear position, vehicle recess measurements, fixing points and nearby sensors or cameras. Follow the process in the UAE number plate holder fit guide.

The approved opening must leave every part of the official plate readable. This is particularly important when the branding band is deeper than a plain frame.

3. Supply usable brand artwork

Send the dealership or marque logo as a vector file such as AI, EPS, SVG or print-ready PDF. Add the official colour references, minimum clear space, approved wording, branch details if required and any co-branding rules.

Do not rely on a low-resolution logo copied from a website. The manufacturer has to translate the artwork into a process that holds fine lines and spacing at a small physical size.

4. Choose the branding process

The right process depends on artwork, quantity, durability and budget. Foil stamping can provide a crisp metallic or high-contrast premium result. Pad printing handles certain detailed or multi-colour graphics. Stickers may suit temporary or very short-life uses but add an exposed edge that must be evaluated.

Ask for process-specific samples instead of approving only a digital mock-up. Our foil, pad printing and sticker comparison explains the trade-offs.

5. Approve a production-intent sample

The approval sample should use the intended polymer, colour, logo process, fixing method and packaging. Install it on the actual vehicle with the correct official plate format. Review fit, readability, logo position, colour, surface finish, closure and removal method.

Record approval with photographs, sample date, drawing revision and the names of the authorised approvers. A signed physical master sample is especially useful for large or recurring orders.

6. Confirm quantity, packaging and delivery

A concise RFQ template

Your request should identify the company, project, vehicle applications, plate formats, estimated volume, branding files, finish, colour, packaging, destination, required sample date and production delivery date. Ask the supplier to separate one-time development or tooling costs from recurring unit and logistics costs.

A complete brief produces a more accurate quotation and reduces the number of approval cycles. It also makes competing offers easier to compare on equal specifications.

Frequently asked questions

What artwork file is best for a branded plate holder?

Vector artwork such as AI, EPS, SVG or a print-ready vector PDF is best. Include official colour references and approved brand spacing.

Should the dealer approve a physical sample?

Yes. Approve a production-intent sample using the intended material, finish and packaging, then fit it to the actual vehicle and plate format.

Can one holder be used across every dealer vehicle?

Sometimes, but it should not be assumed. Front and rear plate formats, recesses and vehicle mounting areas may require different holder variants.

What changes the order lead time?

New tooling, artwork complexity, sampling cycles, material availability, quantity, packaging and multi-branch delivery can all affect timing.
