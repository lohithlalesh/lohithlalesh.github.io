Are Number Plate Frames Legal in the UAE?

Are number plate frames legal in the UAE? Learn the difference between official registration plates and holders, plus visibility and fit requirements.

By MOTTO AutomotiveAugust 16, 20264 min readReviewed August 16, 2026
Home/Insights/UAE Compliance
Quick answer

A number plate holder or frame is not the same as an official registration plate. A correctly fitted accessory frame may be used only when it does not cover, obscure, distort, alter or reduce the readability of the state-issued plate or conflict with its lawful installation. The final responsibility is to comply with current federal law, executive regulations and the relevant emirate licensing authority.

Dealers and drivers often ask whether branded number plate frames are legal in the UAE. The practical answer depends less on the presence of a frame and more on what the installed assembly does. If a frame interferes with an official plate, its data or its required mounting, it creates a compliance risk. This article explains the distinction using current federal legislation and official UAE transport guidance.

Official registration plate versus accessory frame

Important distinction

The official registration plate is issued by a licensing authority and identifies the registered vehicle. A number plate holder or frame is a separately manufactured accessory that secures or surrounds that plate. MOTTO Automotive manufactures holders, not government registration plates.

A decorative or dealer-branded frame does not gain permission to change the official plate. It should be designed around the plate's full visible area and installed without moving, cutting, recolouring, reproducing or covering registration information.

What current UAE traffic legislation says

Federal Decree-Law No. 14 of 2024 On Traffic Regulation is the current federal traffic law identified by the UAE Government. It took effect on 29 March 2025.

  • Article 21 requires vehicles, other than stated exemptions, to have licence plates affixed according to the terms and specifications set by the Executive Regulations.
  • Article 34 addresses serious plate misuse, including forging or imitating a plate, distorting, obliterating or altering plate data, transferring a plate without approval, and installing a plate in violation of the law or regulations.

These provisions focus on the official plate and its lawful use. They do not turn an accessory frame into an official plate, and they do not excuse a frame that makes the official plate unclear or improperly installed.

A practical frame compliance check

Before delivery or installation, inspect the complete assembly in daylight and from normal road-viewing angles:

  1. Every letter, number, colour field, mark and identifier on the official plate remains fully visible.
  2. The frame does not create glare, a false character, a shadow or a surface that could be mistaken for plate data.
  3. The holder secures the plate without bending, trimming, drilling through visible data or changing the plate face.
  4. No cover, film or tinted layer sits over the official plate.
  5. The assembly is securely attached in the intended location and does not obstruct lights, cameras or sensors.
  6. Dealer branding remains outside the official plate's readable field.

Why visibility matters even when carrying accessories

Dubai RTA's guidance for vehicles carrying trunk-mounted bicycles illustrates the same principle. When bicycles or the rack obstruct the rear plate, RTA provides a service for an additional plate to be fixed to the rack. The solution is not to accept an obscured identifier. It is to preserve lawful plate visibility through the authorised process.

The same reasoning should guide frame design. Styling, dealership promotion and mounting convenience are secondary to a clear, correctly installed official plate.

How dealers should specify branded frames

Use a signed drawing that defines the holder's internal opening, branding band and retaining lip. Approve the sample with the actual plate format and the intended vehicle. For multi-emirate or mixed-fleet orders, keep separate approvals where plate formats or mounting areas differ.

Ask the manufacturer to lock the approved artwork and dimensional revision into the production record. Receiving teams can then compare delivered frames with the signed sample rather than making a subjective visual decision.

Who should confirm a specific case

For a particular vehicle, plate category or unusual mounting arrangement, obtain guidance from the licensing authority in the emirate where the vehicle is registered. Dubai customers can use RTA's official vehicle plate services; other emirates have their own competent authorities.

Legal note: This article provides general procurement and design information, not legal advice. Laws, Executive Regulations, service rules and enforcement practices can change. Check the current official sources and the relevant licensing authority before manufacture or installation.

Frequently asked questions

Is a branded number plate frame automatically illegal in the UAE?

Not automatically. The key issues are whether the official plate remains clear, unaltered and correctly installed, and whether the assembly complies with current law, regulations and local authority requirements.

Can a frame cover the edge of a UAE registration plate?

It should not cover any official data, identifier, coloured field or required visible area. Approve the holder against the actual plate format before use.

Can a supplier print a replacement registration plate?

No. An accessory manufacturer should not reproduce or imitate the state-issued registration plate. Replacement official plates must be obtained through the competent licensing authority.

What if a bike rack hides the rear plate?

Use the authorised process. Dubai RTA offers an additional plate service for certain rack situations so the official identifier remains visible.
