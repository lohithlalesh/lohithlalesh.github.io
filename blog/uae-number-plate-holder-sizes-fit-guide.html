UAE Number Plate Holder Sizes: A Fit Guide for Dealers and Fleets

A practical UAE number plate holder size and fit guide for dealers, fleets and accessory buyers covering plate formats, vehicle recesses and approvals.

By MOTTO AutomotiveAugust 16, 20264 min readReviewed August 16, 2026
Home/Insights/Fit Guide
Quick answer

There is no safe one-size-fits-all ordering rule for every UAE vehicle. Licensing services recognise different plate formats, including short and long options, while front and rear mounting areas can differ by vehicle. Dealers and fleets should approve a holder from the actual issued plate format, vehicle recess, fixing points and sensor clearance before volume production.

A number plate holder may look simple, but size errors are expensive at dealer and fleet scale. A frame that is a few millimetres too tight can bow the official plate. One that is too deep may touch a camera or prevent a boot handle from operating. This guide explains how UAE buyers can create a reliable fit specification without assuming that one measurement covers every emirate, plate class and vehicle.

First, separate the official plate from the holder

The official registration plate is issued through the relevant licensing authority and carries the vehicle's registration information. A number plate holder or frame is an accessory that secures or surrounds that plate. The holder must never replace, reproduce or alter the official plate.

That distinction matters when discussing size. Buyers are specifying an accessory around a government-issued item, not choosing the dimensions of the registration plate itself. The frame must preserve a clear view of every character, colour field and identifying detail.

Why one published size is not enough

The UAE has more than one plate format. Dubai's Roads and Transport Authority, for example, lets customers select front and rear plate sizes and refers to short and long plates in its vehicle plate services. Other emirates and vehicle categories may have their own combinations.

Online listings often present one familiar rectangular dimension as a universal answer. That is not a dependable procurement specification. Even when the official plate fits inside a nominal opening, the complete assembly still has to suit the vehicle's recess, mounting centres, curves, tailgate hardware, parking sensors and cameras.

A reliable fit approval workflow

  1. Group the fleet. Create a list by vehicle model, model year, trim, front position and rear position.
  2. Confirm the issued plate formats. Do not infer the format from a photograph or a previous order.
  3. Measure the actual plate and mounting area. Use a drawing with named dimensions rather than measurements in a message thread.
  4. Review the visible opening. Check that the holder cannot cover any official plate data.
  5. Fit a production-intent sample. Install it using the intended clips, screws or mounting system.
  6. Approve with evidence. Keep signed drawings and front, side and rear photographs for each approved application.

For mixed fleets, a small family of holder variants is usually more reliable than forcing one frame onto every vehicle.

Common fit failures to catch before production

  • The frame lip overlaps a digit, category mark or coloured field.
  • The holder sits proud because the rear wall is deeper than the vehicle recess.
  • Screw heads or clips mark the visible face of the official plate.
  • The rear holder blocks a reversing camera, boot release or lamp.
  • The frame twists on a curved bumper and releases one corner.
  • A large dealer branding band reduces the usable plate opening.

A supplier should be able to explain how these risks are checked during design and sampling, not only after delivery.

What to include in a size and fit RFQ

Send the manufacturer a structured pack containing the vehicle matrix, official plate formats, target quantities, artwork, preferred branding process, colour, mounting method, packaging requirement and delivery schedule. State whether one sample is needed per vehicle group and identify who has authority to sign off fit.

For a new programme, ask for the approved drawing revision to appear on the sample record and purchase order. That small control helps purchasing, the showroom and the factory work from the same version.

Frequently asked questions

Is there one standard UAE number plate holder size?

No single holder size should be assumed for every UAE plate and vehicle. Confirm the issued plate format, front or rear position, vehicle recess and fixing points before ordering.

Do short and long number plates need different holders?

Normally, yes. The opening, overall frame dimensions and mounting geometry should be designed for the plate format and the vehicle application.

Can a dealer logo make a plate frame non-compliant?

Branding becomes a problem if the holder covers, obscures or distorts any official plate information. Approve the visible opening with the branding band included.

What is the best way to approve fit for a mixed fleet?

Use a vehicle and plate-format matrix, then install and photograph a production-intent sample for every application group before bulk production.
