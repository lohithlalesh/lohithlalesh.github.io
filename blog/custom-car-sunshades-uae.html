Custom Car Sunshades in the UAE

Choose a custom car sun shade in the UAE by windscreen fit, reflective material, sensor clearance, printed branding, folding, packaging and bulk supply.

By MOTTO AutomotiveAugust 16, 20264 min readReviewed August 21, 2026

Quick answer

For a custom car sun shade in the UAE, specify the vehicle group, windscreen coverage, mirror and sensor clearance, reflective material, printed artwork, folding method, storage and packaging. Test the production-intent car windshield sun shade inside representative vehicles before ordering for a dealer network or mixed fleet.

A branded windscreen sunshade is both a practical accessory and a large visual surface for a dealership. It is seen during vehicle delivery, servicing and everyday parking, but only if customers find it easy to open, position, fold and store. The buying decision therefore starts with fit and usability, then moves to print and promotion.

Start with the vehicle and customer programme

List the vehicle makes, models and model years that will receive the sunshade. Decide whether one adaptable size will cover a broad range or whether premium models need dedicated variants. Note windscreens with large sensor housings, dash cameras, unusual mirror mounts or steep rake angles.

Also define the use: retail gift, showroom add-on, service campaign, fleet issue or paid accessory. The intended use affects construction, packaging and how much time staff can spend during handover.

Approve coverage, fit and storage

A sunshade should cover the useful glass area without forcing material against the mirror, cameras or sensor housing. It needs enough structure to remain in position with the approved support method, yet it should not be awkward to remove or fold.

Check the stored size inside the vehicle. A shade that protects the windscreen but does not fit in the door pocket, seat-back pocket or boot location defined by the dealer may be left at home.

Design the branding for real use

Confirm which face is visible from outside the parked vehicle and how the artwork reads after the shade is unfolded. Keep important wording away from fold lines, edge binding and mirror cut-outs. Large logos need enough resolution and colour control for the selected print process.

Review a printed physical sample in daylight. Reflective and textured surfaces can shift colour and contrast compared with a computer proof.

Run a multi-vehicle sample check

  1. Install the sample in every representative windscreen group.
  2. Check coverage, mirror and sensor clearance, and stability.
  3. Open and fold it several times using normal showroom handling.
  4. Place the stored item in the intended vehicle location.
  5. Inspect print alignment, edge finish and any transfer or marking.
  6. Record approved vehicle applications and sample revision.

Plan a bulk dealer order

Provide expected annual volume, initial launch quantity, branch allocation, carton labels, individual packaging and delivery windows. If multiple artwork versions are required, use clear product codes and define whether mixed cartons are allowed.

Ask the supplier how production variation will be checked for open dimensions, print position, edge finish, fold function and pack count. Receiving teams should have a short inspection sheet and an approved sample.

Common buying mistakes

  • Choosing size from a generic online listing without testing the vehicle group
  • Approving artwork on screen but not on the production material
  • Ignoring mirror and camera housings
  • Making the stored package too large for convenient daily use
  • Ordering one version for all branches without a replenishment plan
  • Making heat-reduction claims that have not been defined or tested

Frequently asked questions

Can one custom sunshade fit every UAE vehicle?

A universal design may suit a broad group, but do not assume complete coverage. Test representative windscreen sizes, mirror housings and sensor areas.

Which side of a car sunshade should carry the dealer logo?

Approve the artwork on the face visible from outside when installed, while keeping the functional reflective orientation specified by the product design.

What artwork should be supplied for a large printed sunshade?

Use high-resolution or vector artwork with official colour references, safe areas and the intended viewing orientation.

How should dealers distribute sunshades to branches?

Use vehicle-delivery forecasts, labelled cartons, defined pack quantities, reorder levels and product codes for each artwork or size variant.
