Custom Showroom Plates for UAE Dealers

Plan custom showroom plates for UAE car dealers, including use cases, dimensions, branding, mounting, finish, samples and the road-use distinction.

By MOTTO AutomotiveAugust 16, 20264 min readReviewed August 16, 2026

Quick answer

A custom showroom plate is a non-registration display accessory for vehicles in controlled settings such as showroom floors, photography bays, launches and exhibitions. It must never be represented as or substituted for an official state-issued registration plate on a vehicle used on public roads.

Display vehicles are photographed, moved between events and viewed from close range. A temporary paper sign or inconsistent plate can weaken an otherwise premium presentation. Custom showroom plates give dealers a reusable format for model names, campaign messages and dealership branding, provided the programme clearly separates display use from official registration.

Showroom display plate versus official registration plate

Display use only

A showroom plate is a branded display sign. It is not issued by a licensing authority, does not register the vehicle and must not imitate, reproduce or replace an official UAE registration plate for road use.

When a vehicle is driven where official registration plates are required, it must carry the authorised plates in the required manner. The display plate should be removed or used only in a location and manner approved by the responsible dealer team.

Where custom showroom plates add value

  • New-model launches and media previews
  • Showroom floor and window displays
  • Approved vehicle photography and video sets
  • Motor shows, exhibitions and private events
  • Used-car presentation with dealer or programme branding
  • Model-name, trim or campaign identification in a controlled display

Build the display plate specification

Define overall dimensions, corner shape, thickness, substrate, finish, artwork, front and rear appearance, edge treatment, mounting method and viewing distance. State whether the plate will be installed for hours, moved daily or reused across a long campaign.

For close-up vehicle photography, surface flatness, clean edges and controlled reflections matter. For events, handling resistance and quick tool-free changes may be more important.

Design for the camera and the showroom

Keep the message short enough to read at the intended distance. Use approved vector logos and define safe areas around fixings, clips and frames. Dark gloss surfaces can reflect lights and people, while highly reflective metallic finishes may distract in photography. Test the chosen finish in the actual environment.

Create separate artwork codes for model names, campaign versions and languages. This reduces the risk of the wrong plate appearing on a vehicle during a launch.

Choose a mounting method that protects the vehicle

The display plate may fit an existing holder, use a concealed temporary mount or attach to an approved display fixture. The method should not scratch paint, load a grille, interfere with sensors or encourage staff to drill unapproved holes.

Provide handling instructions and identify which vehicle applications have been approved. Inspect reusable mounts and plates before every event.

Approve the complete presentation

  1. Install the production-intent plate on the representative vehicle.
  2. Review from showroom viewing distance and through the camera.
  3. Check alignment, reflections, mounting stability and sensor clearance.
  4. Confirm that all wording is display branding, not an imitation of registration data.
  5. Approve protective sleeves, carton labels and branch allocation.

Keep road-use controls clear

Current UAE traffic law requires licence plates to be affixed according to applicable terms and specifications and addresses forging, imitating, altering and unlawfully installing official plates. Dealer operating procedures should make the display-only status unmistakable and assign responsibility for removing display plates before road use.

Legal note: This is general product and procurement information, not legal advice. Confirm any unusual vehicle movement or display arrangement with the relevant licensing and event authorities.

Frequently asked questions

Is a showroom plate an official UAE number plate?

No. It is a non-registration display sign and must not imitate, replace or be represented as an official state-issued registration plate.

Can a showroom plate stay on a car driven on public roads?

Do not use it as a substitute for required official registration plates. Dealer procedures should remove display plates and ensure authorised plates are correctly fitted before road use.

What material is best for a reusable display plate?

The choice depends on viewing distance, finish, mounting, handling and campaign life. Approve a physical sample in the intended showroom or photography environment.

Can showroom plates be made for Arabic and English campaigns?

Yes. Use approved artwork, legibility checks and separate product codes so the correct language and campaign version reaches each display.
