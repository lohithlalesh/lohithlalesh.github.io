A Personal Note from Our Managing Director

Driving Progress. Building the Future.

Meet our Managing Director, Pavle Rastovic, as he shares his perspective on transformation, resilience and opportunity across the Middle East automotive market.

Pavle Rastovic Managing Director
MOTTO Automotive

The automotive industry is entering one of the most transformative periods in its history.

Across the Middle East, we are witnessing the rapid rise of new technologies, new mobility solutions, changing customer expectations and an increasingly competitive global marketplace. At the same time, geopolitical tensions and economic uncertainty continue to test businesses across our region.

Yet, every challenge also brings opportunity.

The Middle East, particularly the UAE, has repeatedly demonstrated resilience, adaptability and a commitment to innovation. These qualities continue to attract investment, accelerate growth and position our region as one of the world's most exciting automotive markets.

Success in the years ahead will not belong to those who wait for stability. It will belong to those who embrace change, build strong partnerships, invest in innovation and continue creating value for their customers.

I remain confident that our industry will not only overcome today's challenges but emerge stronger, smarter and better connected than ever before.

The future of mobility is being shaped today, and the Middle East will play an increasingly important role in driving it forward.
